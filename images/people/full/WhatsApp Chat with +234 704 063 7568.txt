11/16/26, 6:07 PM - +234 704 063 7568: WORSHIP BEYOND JUST THE MUSIC 

Worship is a beautiful expression of our relationship with God. 
It is associated often with music, singing, dancing or sounds that fill the places of worship. 

But worship goes beyond this. Worship is a lifestyle, it involves a posture of your heart and genuine reverence to God. 

John 4:24 says; "God is spirit and His worshippers must worship in spirit and in truth". 
This implies that God is not just interested in our outward expressions, rather He desires a genuine relationship with the heart that bears the expression. 

True worship is not by how we sing beautifully, how we dance or how emotional we get during worship sessions. 
True worship is revealed in the sincerity of our hearts, how intimate we are with God and how consistent our walk is with Him.
Written by Min. Peter Gloria 
ASST. General secretary, PVC.
