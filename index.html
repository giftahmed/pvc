10

Something worth anticipating

10 years of God’s unfailing faithfulness and steadfast love.

Ten years of lives transformed, destinies aligned, hearts revived and Kingdom impact. From where it all began to where God is taking us, what a journey it has been.

This is more than an anniversary. It is a celebration of God’s faithfulness, mercy and favour, and of the countless lives He has touched through PVC.

Our vision & mission

Divinely formed and nurtured for earthly and eternal fulfilment.Isaiah 64:8
  1. Employing and engaging all possible means to understand and walk in the will of God at all time.
  2. Living a life of worship and gratitude to God.
  3. Building a strong connection, bond and care among members to become a family.
  4. Engaging members in corporate and intense prayer activities and Word exposition.
  5. Engaging in corporate financial investment that enhances freedom.
  6. Engaging in career development and support.
  7. Wellbeing and welfare.
